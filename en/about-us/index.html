About us

Clothing that takes your insulin pump into account

Our mission

Living carefree with diabetes

At Diabeticswear we dream of a world in which people with diabetes can live carefree, without their insulin pump getting in the way of their daily lives. Our clothing is designed especially to give them this freedom.

We believe everyone deserves to feel free and confident, whatever challenges diabetes may bring. That is why we keep innovating and strive for the highest quality in everything we do.

What we stand for

Our principles

Freedom of movement

Exercising, working or relaxing: your pump stays securely in place.

No dangling tube

The opening in the pocket keeps the infusion tube neatly tucked away.

Discreet

The tube is tucked away for an inconspicuous look.

Quality

Every garment is carefully tested for comfort and durability.
