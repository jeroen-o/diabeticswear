Description

For people with diabetes who use an insulin pump, everyday comfort is a necessity, not a luxury. This tank top offers a smart, stylish and practical way to carry your pump.

Two integrated pockets on the front hold your insulin pump securely and discreetly, at work, while shopping or relaxing at home. Two subtle openings on the inside route the pump tube neatly to your body, so nothing dangles and everything stays hidden under your clothes.

The tank top is made of 100% cotton for a soft, sturdy fit that is comfortable all day and also at night. The pockets close with Velcro, so your pump stays in place even when you move a lot.

Questions

Frequently asked questions about this product

Will my insulin pump fit in the pockets?

The two pockets on the front are 11.7 × 13.8 cm (w × h) and close with Velcro. They fit most common insulin pumps. Not sure? Email your pump model to info@diabeticswear.com.

How does the infusion tube run?

There are two sturdy, reinforced openings on the inside. This lets you route the tube under the shirt to your pump without anything dangling.

Which size should I choose?

The t-shirts and tank tops run large. We recommend ordering one size smaller than usual. See the size chart on this page.

What is it made of?

100% cotton: soft, breathable and suitable for daily use, also at night.
