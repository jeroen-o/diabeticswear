Description

Often on the go and want to carry your insulin pen and needles safely and neatly? This 3D-printed insulin pen holder has room for 1 insulin pen and 5 needles, so you always have everything at hand without loose items rattling around your bag.

Smart, compact design

The holder has two round openings (both 2 cm in diameter) that hold your pen and needles firmly. At 16.5 cm high, 2.8 cm deep and 4.8 cm wide, it easily fits in a handbag, backpack or pocket.

Made of PLA

The holder is made of PLA (polylactic acid), a bioplastic made from renewable raw materials such as corn and sugar cane. It is sturdy enough for daily use and lightweight.

Please note

  • Do not expose to direct sunlight or high temperatures for long periods
  • Store dry: PLA can absorb moisture and become more brittle
  • Do not overload: hard drops or bending can cause cracks
