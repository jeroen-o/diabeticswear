Description

Our patch plaster is the ideal way to keep your sensor firmly and reliably in place. Made especially for the Freestyle Libre 2, it gives you extra security in all your daily activities, whether you exercise, swim, shower or simply go about your day.

The patch is made of skin-friendly, breathable and transparent material, so it is barely visible on the skin. It feels comfortable, even with prolonged use.

The patch is water- and sweat-resistant, so it stays in place during exercise and contact with water. With 30 pieces per pack, you are covered for a whole year.

Frequently asked questions about this product

Which sensor or pod is this patch for?

This patch plaster is made for the Freestyle Libre 2. Dimensions: 100 x 82 mm. The cut-out leaves your sensor or pod free.

Is the patch water-resistant?

Yes, the patch is water- and sweat-resistant and suitable for exercise, showering and swimming.

How do I use the patch?

Apply the patch over the edge of your sensor or pod on clean, dry skin. Use a new patch with every change.
