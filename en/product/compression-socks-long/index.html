Description

Enjoy everyday comfort with our long compression socks. Thanks to the light compression and the soft, breathable fabric, they feel supportive all day, at work, while travelling, during sport or at home.

The benefits

  • Non-binding, seamless cuff that fits softly without being too tight
  • Seamless design that reduces friction
  • Breathable, soft fabric that keeps your feet dry and fresh
  • Supportive fit for an energetic feeling throughout the day

Who are these socks for?

  • Anyone who stands or sits a lot and wants extra support
  • People who prefer comfortable, non-constricting socks
  • Travellers and athletes who want to keep their legs and feet fresh

Do you have foot complaints? Always consult your doctor or podiatrist about which socks are suitable for you.
