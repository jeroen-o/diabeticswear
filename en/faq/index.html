FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your question? Contact us, we reply within 24 hours.

General

What should I do if there is a problem with my order?

Contact our customer service at info@diabeticswear.com and we will solve it together.

How can I give feedback?

We appreciate your feedback! Send an email to info@diabeticswear.com.

Product information

How do I know which size I need?

Our t-shirt and tank top pages include a size chart. They run large; we recommend ordering one size smaller than usual. Not sure? Contact us for advice.

Will my insulin pump fit in the pockets?

The pockets fit most common insulin pumps. Not sure? Email us your pump model and we will advise you.

Are all products in stock?

If an item is temporarily out of stock, you can still order it. We will ship it as soon as it is available again.

How do I keep my product in good condition?

Follow the care instructions on the label in the product.

Ordering and delivery

Where do you ship to?

We currently ship to the Netherlands and Belgium.

What are the shipping costs?

Within the Netherlands shipping is free from €30; below that it costs €4.25. To Belgium shipping is free from €75; below that it costs €5.25.

How long does delivery take?

We aim to deliver your order within 2-3 working days.

Can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a tracking number by email.

Payment and returns

Which payment methods do you accept?

iDEAL, Bancontact, Klarna and bank transfer.

How do I return an item?

You can return products within 14 days of receipt. Email info@diabeticswear.com with the reason for the return and we will send you the next steps.

When will I get my money back?

Once we have received and processed your return, we refund the amount within 5 working days.

What are the return costs?

Return shipping is at your own expense, unless the item was incorrectly delivered or defective.

Not sure about your size or pump?

Send us your usual size or the type of insulin pump. We are happy to help and reply within 24 hours.
